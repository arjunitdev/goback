## Not reproduced exactly
- **Footer position:** in 2013 the links sat in the page flow under the box; modern Google anchors its footer to the bottom of the window. The footer is restyled (blue 13px links) but stays at the bottom. Modern items with no 2013 equivalent (the AI promo line, "How Search works", Settings) are kept, not hidden.
- **Services bar:** "Search Images Maps Play YouTube News Gmail Drive" are links the extension adds (`src/adapters/google/chrome.ts`); the "More" menu is not faked. Google's own About / Store / Gmail / Images / apps / Sign in stay, restyled onto the same bar, so Gmail and Images appear twice.
- **Doodles:** the home logo image is replaced by the classic logo, including on days with a doodle. The image still links where it did.
- **Dark and light:** Google picks its theme on the server (seen: a dark first visit, light afterwards, in one browser profile). Authentic mode pins 62 generated variables to light (`scripts/gen-google-theme.mjs`). A few hard-coded dark colours are not variables (the suggestion list) and are overridden directly.
- **Results pages:** NOT verified live (Google shows a CAPTCHA to automated browsers). They receive the light pin and the earlier inferred styles only.
