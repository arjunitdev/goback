Rules
- Archived captures (Wayback etc.) are never copied into `src/`. They belong in `evidence-vault/` (git-ignored).
- User-supplied private assets, if ever used, live in `src/assets/private/` (git-ignored) and must not be packaged.
- `scripts/audit-package.mjs` fails if any wordmark/SVG appears in the `store` artifact.
