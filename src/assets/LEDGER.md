A-005/A-006/A-007 are in `src/assets/private/` (git-ignored, never committed or pushed). `scripts/build.mjs` does not read that folder for the `store` flavor, `src/shared/registration.ts` refuses to register it for `store`, and `scripts/audit-package.mjs` fails the store build if any private or non-icon asset appears.

Rules

- Archived captures (Wayback etc.) are never copied into `src/`. They belong in `evidence-vault/` (git-ignored).
- User-supplied private assets, if ever used, live in `src/assets/private/` (git-ignored) and must not be packaged.
- `scripts/audit-package.mjs` fails if any wordmark/SVG appears in the `store` artifact.
